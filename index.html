Wi-Fi Direct Tethering

Creates a direct peer-to-peer connection instead of a carrier-detectable mobile hotspot. Carriers see regular phone data, not tethering traffic.

🔄

Dual Mode

Switch between Host mode (share your connection) and Client mode (connect to another host). One app, two roles.

💻

Windows Desktop Client

Full-featured Windows app with TUN virtual adapter, routing 100% of traffic through the phone's proxy. Great for gaming.

🎮

Console & TV Support

Bridge your PC's connection to PS5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, Mac, and Smart TVs via Windows Internet Connection Sharing.

🔒

No Root Required

Works entirely within Android's standard VPNService and Wi-Fi Direct APIs. No root access needed.

⚡

Persistent Connection

Foreground service with wake locks, Wi-Fi locks, and auto-restart keeps the connection alive even with the screen off.
